Downloads the soft-masked GRCh38 primary assembly from Ensembl and NCBI BLAST+, then builds a nucleotide database. You can keep using the tool while this runs.

What local data unlocks

  • In-silico PCR. Every primer is BLASTed genome-wide; hits whose 3' ends anneal are paired into predicted products, so you see extra bands before you order.
  • BLAST/BLAT searches. BLASTN, BLASTX, BLASTP, TBLASTN, TBLASTX and BLAT against genomes, cDNAs, ncRNAs and proteins.
  • Offline use. Reference sequence comes from disk rather than the network.

BLAST/BLAT species databases

Each species can have a genome (searched as-is, hard-masked or soft-masked), cDNA, ncRNA and protein databases, gene annotation for overlapping genes and exon markup, a repeat filter for queries, and a BLAT index. Everything comes from Ensembl's current release and installs in the background.
